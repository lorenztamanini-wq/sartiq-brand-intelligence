**1 · WHO**
OVS S.p.A. is Italy's leading value/mass apparel retailer for men, women and children (also underwear, beauty, home decor), running a vertically integrated model across a portfolio of proprietary brands. Listed on Euronext Milan since 2015. Tier: MASS — live PDP price points observed €7–30. Channel mix: DTC-led via 2,600+ stores (primarily OVS, Upim, Goldenpoint banners) plus e-commerce and franchise/wholesale abroad. Standalone group (no parent); banners include PIOMBO, OVS Kids, Blukids, Stefanel, Les Copains, Shaka, CROFF. Markets: Italy core, with franchise expansion in Japan, Mexico and India. _[OVS Group | OVS Corporate <https://www.ovscorporate.it/en/about-us/ovs-group>; OVS Investor Overview <https://www.ovscorporate.it/en/investors/overview>; ovs.it live PDP (t-shirt/top category JSON-LD) <https://www.ovs.it/it/it/c/donna/abbigliamento/t-shirt-polo-e-top/>]_

### THE GAP WE FILL ░ human ░ ✓ confirmed ~est
THROUGHPUT and UNIFORMITY at studio scale — not baseline quality, and emphatically not cost. OVS runs Italy's largest, fastest-turning value catalog through an in-house studio that is effectively at capacity. The live audit proves the pain: on-model/still-life coverage is solid but per-SKU depth swings from 2 to 7 shots and backgrounds alternate between studio grey and plain white across SKUs (medium consistency). The single wedge is elastic AI on-model + still-life capacity that (a) absorbs new-arrival surges the in-house studio can't flex to, (b) standardizes shot-count and background across the OVS/Upim/Goldenpoint/PIOMBO banners, and (c) spins up marketplace and localized franchise-market variants at near-zero marginal cost. A 'cut your photo budget' framing misses — their in-house per-image cost is already low.

### STRATEGY ░ human ░ ✓ confirmed ~est
Scale-the-studio play, not replace-the-studio. Brand and group are aligned and both GROWING (FY2025 sales +7% to €1.75bn, EBITDA +20%), so this is a velocity/innovation pitch, not a turnaround or a cost-cutting wedge. Lead with the constraint: OVS ships more new SKUs per week than almost any Italian retailer and the in-house studio — not the budget — is the bottleneck on new-arrival velocity. Sartiq adds surge capacity that frees the studio to lead on craft, standardizes uniformity across banners, and unlocks the concrete localization lever: Japan/Mexico/India franchise imagery at near-zero marginal cost. Ride their existing digital/AI investment narrative (the €33m Puglia innovation hub) rather than proposing a visual overhaul — position Sartiq as the content-tooling layer that fits the transformation they're already funding.
