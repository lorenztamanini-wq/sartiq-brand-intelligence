**1 · WHO**
Diesel is a denim-led Italian fashion brand (founded 1978, Renzo Rosso) positioned explicitly as "the true alternative to luxury" — accessible-luxury / affordable-luxury tier, not mass. Under creative director Glenn Martens (since 2020) it has repositioned around denim, utility and Y2K pop aesthetics targeting a youth / digital-native audience. It is the founding brand of OTB Group (Breganze, Italy), alongside Maison Margiela, Marni, Jil Sander, Viktor&Rolf, plus Staff International (production/logistics, Dsquared2 licence), Brave Kid (kids licensees) and a stake in Amiri. Channel mix is shifting toward DTC: group retail/outlet/online now ~60% of turnover, with a sizeable but declining wholesale channel. Global footprint (~600 group directly-operated stores); strategic markets include Japan (27.4% of group business), North America, Middle East, plus new market entries (Mexico, Korea). _[Glenn Martens on Diesel Fall 2024 (WWD) <https://wwd.com/fashion-news/designer-luxury/diesel-glenn-martens-fall-2024-collection-preview-1236202470/>; OTB presents its 2025 full-year results <https://www.otb.net/en/news/otb-presents-its-2025-full-year-results>; Otb Group closes 2025 (Il Sole 24 Ore) <https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/otb-group-closes-2025-17-billion-revenue-record-profitability-diesel-AI4EKVSB>]_

### THE GAP WE FILL ░ human ░ ✓ confirmed ~est
Diesel and OTB need OPPOSITE things from the same supplier — and Sartiq is the one partner who plays both. Diesel the brand is winning (best profitability in a decade, youth-led, digital-native) yet its PDP grid contradicts its own campaigns: measured audits show a uniform 6-shot, plain-white studio packshot template with NO video and NO lifestyle imagery, while the brand's marketing is bold, irreverent and Gen-Z. That is the concrete wedge — the PDP grid is a decade behind the campaign energy. Meanwhile OTB the parent is margin-crushed (EBIT collapsed ~77% to ~€10.1m, wholesale in rationalization) and needs consistent assets across five maisons, Brave Kid licensees and a shrinking wholesale channel at commodity cost. One supplier: premium AI on-model + video + localized variants to keep Diesel's PDP as bold as its campaigns, and commodity-cost consistency to stop the bleed for the group.
