1Who
OVS S.p.A. is Italy's leading value/mass apparel retailer (menswear, womenswear, kidswear, plus underwear, beauty and home decor), running a vertically integrated model across the full value chain with a platform of proprietary brands. Tier: MASS / value (price points e.g. dresses €15–35 for the OVS core line, with premium sub-brands like GAP/PIOMBO reaching €80–100). Channel mix: DTC-led via 2,600+ stores (mostly OVS, Upim, Goldenpoint banners) plus e-commerce and franchise/wholesale. Markets: Italy core, with international franchise expansion (Japan, Mexico, India, Dubai). Listed on Euronext Milan since 2015; standalone group (no parent), >9,000 employees. Own-brand portfolio includes PIOMBO, OVS Kids, Blukids, Stefanel, Les Copains, Shaka and CROFF.

The gap we fill ░ human ░

THROUGHPUT, not quality — and that's the whole point with OVS. They already shoot a clean, on-model-led studio set (~5 AI-amenable shots/SKU, ~67% on-model, consistent grey/white backgrounds) in-house, so 'cut your photo budget' is the wrong wedge: per-image cost is already low. The real constraint is volume velocity — ~3,563 live women's-apparel SKUs and an estimated ~40k new SKUs/yr feeding an in-house, vertically integrated studio that is structurally at capacity, while three banners (OVS/Upim/Goldenpoint) plus franchise markets (Japan, Mexico, India, Dubai) each need their own variants. Sartiq's single wedge: elastic AI on-model + still-life capacity that absorbs new-arrival surges and produces marketplace/banner/localized variants at near-zero marginal cost, freeing the studio to lead on craft instead of grinding on volume.

Strategy ░ human ░

Scale-the-studio play (mass/value retailer, brand and group aligned, both GROWING — OVS is standalone, no parent split). Do NOT pitch a visual overhaul or cost displacement: their imagery is already good and their studio is in-house, so the modeled €8.0–47.2M is an agency-rate upper bound that overstates pure savings. Sell speed and localization, not savings. Position Sartiq as overflow capacity on top of an already-good visual system. Anchor credibility to OVS's own €33m Puglia digital/AI hub — they're already buying into AI-enabled operations, so this is an extension of stated strategy, not a leap. Open the relationship on new-arrival surge velocity and zero-marginal-cost localization across banners and franchise markets as the concrete, sizable levers.
