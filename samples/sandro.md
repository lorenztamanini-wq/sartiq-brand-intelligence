### THE GAP WE FILL ░ human ░ ~est
Sandro's brand equity lives in consistent, accurate on-model imagery — and that consistency breaks the moment it hits wholesale and marketplace channels. The gap is guaranteed garment fidelity at scale across every channel, which generic self-serve AI cannot promise.
> ⟲ **AI draft —** Sandro could save money on photography by switching to AI imagery.
>
> ░ human ░ **why I changed it —** Wrong lead for full-price accessible-luxury — price is the second sentence, never the first. The wedge is brand-consistent garment FIDELITY across every channel (which generic self-serve AI can't promise); off-brand marketplace imagery is markdown risk, and that is the real money.


### STRATEGY ░ human ░ ~est
consistency-led (premium); ROI/efficiency without quality loss (profit focus)
