# BRAND BRIEF — Sandro

**Opportunity 3.2/5** · Accessible-luxury Parisian ready-to-wear; full-price strategy
Parent: SMCP Group (Sandro, Maje, Claudie Pierlot, Fursac) · Markets: France core; global DTC + wholesale; ~1,600 stores (group)
Partners / sister brands: Maje, Claudie Pierlot, Fursac, wholesale partners in new markets
---

**1 · WHO**
Sandro is an accessible-luxury Parisian ready-to-wear brand (Women & Men), the largest brand in the SMCP group (alongside Maje, Claudie Pierlot and Fursac). Positioning is premium "Parisian chic" priced below true luxury — live PDPs show ~$229 loafers, ~$525 leather bags and ~$1,130 leather jackets. Channel mix is DTC-led (own e-commerce + directly operated stores) with selective and growing wholesale/partner distribution. SMCP operates 1,600+ stores across 59 countries; Sandro alone had 547 directly operated points of sale (H1 2025). Founded 1984 by Evelyne Chetrite, who still provides creative direction. _[SMCP 2025 FY Results press release (GlobeNewswire) <https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/26/3245863/0/en/SMCP-2025-FY-Results.html>; SMCP Interim Financial Report H1 2025 <https://www.smcp.com/app/uploads/2025/07/interim-financial-report-2025.pdf>; us.sandro-paris.com live PDPs <https://us.sandro-paris.com>]_

**2 · DIRECTION**
AHEAD ON CRAFT — lead with consistency, not innovation-for-its-own-sake. Sandro's product imagery has been a tightly art-directed house look for years (Wayback homepage snapshots 2022→2026 confirm a stable premium aesthetic), and the live PDP audit confirms the same disciplined studio system today: clean plain-white / studio-grey backgrounds, on-model + detail + ghost-mannequin packshot for apparel, consistent still-life for accessories, and product video now embedded on apparel PDPs (VideoObject in PDP JSON-LD, uploaded 2025–2026). The trigger to watch is channel expansion: SMCP is aggressively adding wholesale/marketplace partners (10 new countries in 2025; Samsung distributing SMCP in South Korea from SS26), which is exactly where a tightly-controlled house look is hardest to hold. The opening is protecting that consistency across channels, not proposing a new direction — off-brand partner imagery is the risk. _[Wayback snapshots — sandro-paris.com homepage 2022 vs 2026 <https://web.archive.org/web/20220106143233/http://sandro-paris.com/>; Live PDP audit — video embedded on apparel <https://us.sandro-paris.com/en/p/smooth-leather-jacket/SFPBL01286_20.html>; SMCP Q3 2025 results (WWD) <https://wwd.com/business-news/financial/smcp-q3-2025-sales-growth-us-expansion-full-price-strategy-1238321928/>]_

**5 · ON THE PDPs**
Measured from a live audit of 4 PDPs across categories + classification of 11 primary images. Shots per SKU run 5–9 (bag 8 hi-res + packshot; jacket 6 + video + packshot; loafers 4 + packshot; bodysuit 5 + video + packshot). Image system is category-specific and highly disciplined: APPAREL = on-model lead shot + on-model detail crops + a ghost-mannequin/packshot, plus an embedded product video (Vimeo, in JSON-LD). ACCESSORIES (bags, shoes) = clean still-life/packshot on plain-white or studio-grey, no model. Across the classified sample the mix skewed still-life ~55% / on-model ~27% / detail + ghost-mannequin the remainder because accessories dominated the sample; on the apparel PDPs specifically the lead imagery is consistently on-model. Art direction is tight and coherent on the DTC site — consistency is the brand asset. The known weak point (not yet audited here) is wholesale/marketplace partner pages, where imagery is frequently downgraded or off-brand — a concrete, demoable gap. _[classify_images — 11 Sandro PDP images <https://us.sandro-paris.com>; audit_pdp — jacket / bag / loafers / bodysuit <https://us.sandro-paris.com/en/p/le-mambo-suede-bag/SFASA01638_20.html>]_

### THE GAP WE FILL ░ human ░ ✓ confirmed ~est
Sandro's entire equity is a tightly art-directed house look — on-model apparel, clean still-life accessories, product video — held with real discipline on its own DTC PDPs. That control breaks the instant product lands on wholesale and marketplace partner pages, and SMCP is expanding precisely that footprint right now (10 new partner countries in 2025, Samsung distributing in South Korea from SS26). Under Sandro's strict full-price strategy (~20% discount rate), every downgraded or off-brand partner image is direct markdown risk. THE ONE GAP Sartiq fills: guaranteed garment fidelity and Sandro's house art-direction reproduced at catalog scale across every channel and every localized market — the one promise generic self-serve AI imagery cannot make.

### STRATEGY ░ human ░ ✓ confirmed ~est
Consistency-led, group-scale. Do NOT pitch a new visual direction (they are ahead on craft) and do NOT open on cost. Lead with protecting brand-consistent garment fidelity across the expanding partner/marketplace footprint, framed as markdown-risk reduction under the full-price strategy — the argument a premium, margin-recovering brand actually feels. Second beat only: same house quality at a fraction of studio cost and time, plus localization at near-zero marginal cost as SMCP adds markets. Sell once at group level — a single relationship spans Sandro, Maje, Claudie Pierlot and Fursac, turning a ~€1.58m brand opportunity into ~€4.75m of portfolio upside. Note the ownership SPLIT: SMCP's majority holders are exploring a capital transaction, so keep the pitch operational (to the digital/image owner) and avoid tying it to shareholder-level timing.
