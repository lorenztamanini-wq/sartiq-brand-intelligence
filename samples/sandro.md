**1 · WHO**
Sandro is an accessible-luxury Parisian ready-to-wear house (founded 1984 by Evelyne Chetrite, who still provides creative direction) and the largest brand within SMCP Group, alongside Maje, Claudie Pierlot and Fursac. Tier: accessible-luxury — live PDPs show full-price points of ~$300–480 (e.g. $480 knit dress, $315 polo). Channel mix is DTC-led (own eshops across many country/market sites: US, FR, etc.) plus selective wholesale and marketplace partners; the group runs a strict full-price strategy. SMCP is a global accessible-luxury leader present in 59 countries with 1,600+ stores; the group is listed on Euronext Paris (ticker SMCP). _[SMCP 2025 FY Results press release <https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/26/3245863/0/en/SMCP-2025-FY-Results.html>; Sandro US PDP (JSON-LD price/positioning) <https://us.sandro-paris.com/en/p/ribbed-knit-midi-dress/SFPRO04930_20.html>]_

**2 · DIRECTION**
AHEAD ON CRAFT — lead with consistency, not innovation-for-its-own-sake. Wayback then-vs-now shows Sandro has run a tightly art-directed, on-model studio house look for years: the 2022 snapshot already presents a polished seasonal-collection eshop (titled "Fall/Winter 2021 Collection"), and the live 2026 PDPs show the same disciplined studio-grey on-model + still-life + per-PDP video standard. So the brand is not visually behind and does not need a new creative direction — the opening is protecting that hard-won consistency across channels (off-brand wholesale/marketplace imagery is markdown risk), not proposing reinvention. No new-creative-director/rebrand/replatform trigger observed; platform remains Salesforce Commerce Cloud (demandware). _[Wayback snapshot — Sandro eshop, Jan 2022 <https://web.archive.org/web/20220106143233/http://sandro-paris.com/>; Live Sandro PDP image classification (2026) <https://us.sandro-paris.com/en/p/ribbed-knit-midi-dress/SFPRO04930_20.html>]_

### THE GAP WE FILL ░ human ░ ~est
Sandro's equity is its tightly art-directed, studio-grey on-model house look — and that consistency shatters the moment product crosses into wholesale and marketplace channels, where partners show downgraded or off-brand shots. The single gap Sartiq fills is guaranteed garment fidelity at catalog scale held to Sandro's own art direction across EVERY channel and every localized country eshop — the one promise self-serve AI tools can't make. For a strict full-price house (in-season discount capped ~20%), each off-brand partner image is direct markdown risk, not a cosmetic nit.

### STRATEGY ░ human ░ ~est
Win it consistency-first, group-wide. Lead with brand-consistent, garment-accurate on-model + still-life imagery held to Sandro's standard across DTC, wholesale, marketplace and localized markets; make price the second sentence, never the first — framed as ROI/efficiency without quality loss, matching SMCP's FY2025 profit-and-cash turnaround (EBIT +80%, record €91m FCF). Sell one relationship across all four SMCP brands (Sandro, Maje, Claudie Pierlot, Fursac) to size the partner upside, not the single brand. Quantify the localization lever explicitly — localized imagery at near-zero marginal cost across SMCP's 59-country eshop footprint is a concrete number to put on the table. Do NOT pitch reinvention: there is no new-CD/rebrand/replatform trigger and the brand is already ahead on craft — the play is protecting hard-won consistency, not proposing a new look.
