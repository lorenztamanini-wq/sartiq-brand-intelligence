Diesel SPLIT
Denim-led, youth, repositioned as 'the true alternative to luxury' · OTB Group (Diesel, Margiela, Marni, Jil Sander, Viktor&Rolf + Brave Kid)
Gap — Diesel and OTB need OPPOSITE things from the same supplier — and no single vendor is currently serving both registers. The Diesel brand is winning (best profitability in a decade, youth-led, Berlin/Seoul flagships, new market entries) and is buying INNOVATION: distinctive AI on-model imagery to feed its digital-native DTC push, localized at near-zero marginal cost across Japan (~27% of group sales), NA (+5.9%) and Middle East (+9%). The OTB parent is buying EFFICIENCY: operating profit collapsed ~77%, wholesale down ~15%, margins hit by write-downs from new creative directors at Margiela/Jil Sander/Marni. The concrete wedge: Diesel's own PDPs are near-perfectly consistent (95.8% on-model, plain white, ~4 shots/SKU), but that coherence does NOT travel to the wholesale/marketplace channel (Farfetch et al.) or sister maisons. One partner plays both: premium AI imagery for the brand, commodity-cost consistency for the group's maisons, Brave Kid licensees, and a still-large but shrinking wholesale channel that needs cheap, on-brand assets.

OVS GROWING (brand and group aligned — OVS is standalone, so no brand-vs-parent split). FY2025 (year to Jan 2026)
Italy's leading value/mass apparel retailer, vertically integrated · OVS S.p.A. (standalone, Euronext Milan)
Gap — THROUGHPUT, not quality — and that's the whole point with OVS. They already shoot a clean, on-model-led studio set (~5 AI-amenable shots/SKU, ~67% on-model, consistent grey/white backgrounds) in-house, so 'cut your photo budget' is the wrong wedge: per-image cost is already low. The real constraint is volume velocity — ~3,563 live women's-apparel SKUs and an estimated ~40k new SKUs/yr feeding an in-house, vertically integrated studio that is structurally at capacity, while three banners (OVS/Upim/Goldenpoint) plus franchise markets (Japan, Mexico, India, Dubai) each need their own variants. Sartiq's single wedge: elastic AI on-model + still-life capacity that absorbs new-arrival surges and produces marketplace/banner/localized variants at near-zero marginal cost, freeing the studio to lead on craft instead of grinding on volume.

Sandro RECOVERING (with a SPLIT nuance to flag). SMCP Group returned to profit in FY2025
Accessible-luxury Parisian ready-to-wear; full-price strategy · SMCP Group (Sandro, Maje, Claudie Pierlot, Fursac)
Gap — Sandro's equity is its tightly art-directed, studio-grey on-model house look — and that consistency shatters the moment product crosses into wholesale and marketplace channels, where partners show downgraded or off-brand shots. The single gap Sartiq fills is guaranteed garment fidelity at catalog scale held to Sandro's own art direction across EVERY channel and every localized country eshop — the one promise self-serve AI tools can't make. For a strict full-price house (in-season discount capped ~20%), each off-brand partner image is direct markdown risk, not a cosmetic nit.
